# Code of conduct

Emergence Lab is a project about curiosity: watching simple rules produce
surprising behaviour. We want the community around it to be welcoming to
everyone who shares that curiosity, whatever their background or experience.

This code of conduct is based on the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

* Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and their questions.
* Discuss ideas, code and evidence, not people.
* Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
* Credit other people's work, patterns and research properly.
* Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
* Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
* Publishing other people's private information without their permission.
* Spam, including promotional content unrelated to the project.
* Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space (issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer,
[@Ale-Newport](https://github.com/Ale-Newport), through the e-mail address on
their GitHub profile, or use GitHub's *Report content* feature. All reports
are reviewed promptly and confidentially.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that break this code. Depending on the severity, the
response ranges from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from
the project.
