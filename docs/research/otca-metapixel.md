Items marked **UNVERIFIED** are interpretations that were not tested.

The pattern data lives in `assets/patterns/otca/`. See `PROVENANCE.md` there,
and `otca-spec.json`, which is the machine-readable version of sections 4–7 and

## 2. Geometry and coordinates

### 2.1 Tile-nominal coordinates (used throughout)

- **Nominal square.** A tile's nominal square is `[0, 2047]²`. Its RLE files are
  2058 × 2058 with no `#CXRLE Pos`, so they load with the top-left at (0,0).
  **file coordinate = nominal + 5**.
- **Metafier uses the same frame.** `metafier` loads the files with
  `transform(-5, -5)` and composes tiles directly in nominal coordinates.
- **Only 28 cells lie outside the nominal square** (verified):
  - **4 tubs** (`bo$obo$bo!`). Each tub sits *inside the diagonal neighbour*.
    - NW tub: `(-5,-4) (-4,-5) (-4,-3) (-3,-4)`
    - NE tub: `(2050,-4) (2051,-5) (2051,-3) (2052,-4)`
    - SE tub: `(2050,2051) (2051,2050) (2051,2052) (2052,2051)`
    - SW tub: `(-5,2051) (-4,2050) (-4,2052) (-3,2051)`
  - **4 corner blocks** that straddle the tile corners: `(-1..0, -1..0)`,
    `(2047..2048, -1..0)`, `(2047..2048, 2047..2048)` and `(-1..0, 2047..2048)`.
    Only one cell of each block is inside the nominal square.

### 2.2 Tiling (validated two ways)

- **Method A: OR-tiling.** This is what `metafier` does and what we recommend.
  - Place a full 2058×2058 tile with its nominal origin at
    `(2048·i, 2048·j)` for tile column *i* and row *j*. Take the **union** of
    all cells.
  - There is no stagger and no per-row offset. `metafier` adds a global −5
    shift, `put(2048*i-5, 2048*j-5)`, which is irrelevant.
  - Neighbouring tiles overlap by 5 cells. The four corner blocks of the four
    tiles meeting at a corner coincide exactly.
  - Each tile's corner tub lands at `(2..4, 2..4)`-style positions inside its
    diagonal neighbour. Example: the SE tub of tile (i−1, j−1) becomes the tub
    centred at nominal (3,3) of tile (i, j).
- **Method B: trimmed tiling.** This is what Brice's `tile-otcamp.pl`, and
  therefore the Golly demos, do.
  - Each tile is the 2048×2048 trimmed square, a quadtree **level-11 node**.
    The corner blocks are removed.
  - A tub is added at the corner region of a tile *iff* the cross-corner
    neighbour at that corner exists. The tubs are centred at nominal (3,3),
    (2044,3), (2044,2044) and (3,2044) for the NW, NE, SE and SW neighbours.
  - The script ships 32 root variants: {ON, OFF} × 16 corner-neighbour
    presence combinations.
- **The two methods agree (verified).**
  - Every level-11 node of all three Golly demos equals, cell for cell, Brice's
    tile cropped to `[0,2047]²`, plus the rule-programming cells, plus tubs for
    the existing diagonal neighbours, minus the 4 corner cells (0,0), (0,2047),
    (2047,0) and (2047,2047).
  - The corner blocks are inert still lifes.
  - 3×3, 2×2, 1×3 and 1×1 grids built with Method A behave identically
    (section 8).
- **Golly macrocell placement.** Golly's `hlifealgo` places the root of level L
  at x ∈ [−2^(L−1), 2^(L−1)−1] and y ∈ [1−2^(L−1), 2^(L−1)]. This was verified
  with `bgolly edges`. In Brice's `.mc` tilings, tile (i, j) therefore has its
  top-left at `(−2^(L−1) + 2048·i, 1 − 2^(L−1) + 2048·j)`. For the galaxy demo,
  L = 15, which gives (−16384 + 2048i, −16383 + 2048j).
- **HashLife tip.** Put nominal tile origins on multiples of 2048 in the
  engine's quadtree frame. Every tile is then exactly one level-11 node and
  hashing becomes extremely effective. That is why Brice built his tilings this
  way.

### 2.3 Phase and synchronisation requirements

- **Build every tile at generation 0 from the base files.** The OFF and ON files
  are mutually in phase. They differ only in state-dependent parts: the
  state-bit boat and the display. All the P46 and P184 machinery shares phase.
  `metafier` builds the ON display as `CellCenter[3680]`, where
  3680 = 80·46 = 20·184, so the oscillators stay in phase.
- **Do not mix tiles taken at different generations.** Do not add tiles to a
  running pattern either. The first cycle "burns" the proximity fuses between
  neighbours (section 3, item 1). A tile inserted later would need the matching
  `t mod P` phase and pre-burned fuses. That case is untested and
  **UNVERIFIED**.
- **"Tiles must be programmed identically (ON and OFF) before tiling."** This
  is Brice's own rule. Every tile in a pattern gets the same rule bits and the
  same neighbourhood fuses.

## 3. What the tile contains

This section uses the `metafier` subpattern names and Brice's slide captions.
Coordinates are tile-nominal positions of the subpattern's RLE origin, as given
in `metafier.lua`. Mechanism descriptions in *italics* are interpretations of
the slide captions and are **UNVERIFIED** in their detail. Section 4 onward is
verified by simulation.

1. **Slide 21, "Proximity Fuses for 8 Input/Output Channels NW,N,NE,E,SE,S,SW,W"
   and "RULE".**
   - **Corner fuses** (×4, rotations about the tile centre by
     `(x,y)→(2047−y, x)`). Each consists of:
     - an LWSS `b4o$o3bo$4bo$o2bo!` at (8,0), heading west;
     - a `DiagProximityFuse` at (−5,−5): tub, corner block, and a fuse chain
       along the edge.
   - **Edge fuses** (×4). Each is an `OrthoProximityFuse` at (1001,0): a block
     at (1001..1002, 0..1) plus an eater at (1042..1045, 0..3).
   - **How the fuses open the channels.** When a neighbour tile is present, the
     neighbour's tub, block or eater sits right next to these objects. The pair
     reacts during the first cycle and opens the glider channel. Brice's
     header: *"These channels are initially closed by eaters. The physical
     presence of a neighbor tile will trigger the appropriate proximity fuses
     and open the necessary glider channels."*
   - **At the outer border of a finite grid the fuses never burn.** The
     channels stay closed and nothing leaks out.
   - **Rule table** in the SW quadrant:
     - 18 *broken* eaters (`o$3o$3bo$2bo!`). The B column is at
       (145, 1401+o) and the S column is the 180°-rotated copy at
       (165, 1521−o).
     - 18 beehives ("honey bits") at (123..125, 1384+o..1387+o) and (180..182, 1418+o..1421+o).
       *They are probably part of the table-reading logic.*
     - Decorative letters and digits ("RULE", "B", "S", "ON/OFF", "0..8"),
       drawn with isolated cells that die at generation 1.
2. **Slide 22, linear clock ("EVENT TAPS: lwss packet gun, neighbor count
   decode, read B/S rule results, initiate logic cascade").**
   - A glider stream runs up the west side, from `LinearClockGun` at (46,1924)
     (pre-evolved 1840 generations) to `LinearClockCap` at (83,401).
   - It is lined with eaters at x ≈ 101, y = 422…1900. *This is the master
     sequencer for each cycle.*
3. **Slide 23, MWSS track.** An MWSS at (521,1973) circulates around the tile
   perimeter; the slide's arrow runs counter-clockwise.
   - "**Boat-Bit Holding Inverted Cell State**": a boat at (569..571,
     1970..1972), from `StateBit` `b2o$obo$bo!` at (569,1970). It is present in
     the OFF tile and **absent in the ON tile**.
   - "**Logic Core Latch for Cell State**".
   - Signalling mechanisms (`ReflectMWSS`, `GliderFromTheBlue`, `HoneyBit`,
     `CornerSignalSystem`) are placed on three sides at 90° rotations. The west
     side is special: *"signal stops there"*.
   - *The circulating MWSS triggers the glider emissions into the 8 output
     channels.*
4. **Slide 24, "LWSS Train"** circulating around the tile. Four "LWSS Color
   Adjusters" (`PhaseAdjuster`: P46 guns plus eaters) correct the LWSS
   colour/phase. `LWSSPacketGun` sits at (108,710). *Incoming neighbour gliders
   are converted to LWSSs, so the train carries the neighbour count.*
5. **Slide 25, decode.**
   - "sync buffer" around (114..190, 958..1040).
   - "p46 to p40 converter", described as "really 14 p184 guns", at (116,1059).
   - "lookup tables", which is the rule table.
   - "counterflow lwss".
   - *The neighbour-count packet is decoded into a position along the table,
     and the eater present or absent at that row decides the B and S results.*
6. **Slide 26, "Logic Core Latches for B/S Results"**, around (108,1317) and
   along the bottom edge.
7. **Slide 27, boat logic.** It holds the truth table that is the whole
   metapixel "CPU". With **C** the current state, **B** the birth-table result,
   **S** the survival-table result, and **T** the toggle:

   `G = ¬S ∧ C`, `H = B ∧ ¬C`, `T = G ∨ H`.

   So the cell toggles iff (ON and not S) or (OFF and B). A second gate table
   (E, F, G, H → D) handles "CLEANUP D=1".
8. **Slide 28, "LWSS Clears Leftover Boats" and "Logic Timing Signal Triggers
   Next Distribution Cycle".**
9. **Slide 29, toggle distribution.** Boat latches sit near the NW corner
   (`BoatLatchNE` at (120,232)) and the SE corner (`BoatLatchSW` at
   (1534,1849)), along with a "sync buffer" and the "cell state boat-bit".
10. **Slide 30, HWSS control, "Synchronized Glider Guns Poll Latches".**
    - There are two HWSS guns. One is at (106,87) and the other is its mirror
      image under `(x,y)→(2047−y, 2047−x)`.
    - Each feeds a row of 43 P46 "LWSS from the blue" oscillators spaced 40
      apart: along the top edge at (188+40·i, 79), and mirrored down the right
      edge.
11. **Slide 31, display on.**
    - The ON display is 43 nested L-shaped LWSS lanes, 1720 = 43·40 wide.
      Columns run down from the top-edge oscillators and rows run west from the
      right-edge oscillators.
    - In `metafier`, ON = OFF circuitry + `CellCenter` + blocks at (171,124) and
      (1922,1875). Those blocks switch on both HWSS guns. The result is then
      evolved 3680 generations to fill the display.
    - The display switches at LWSS speed c/2. Brice: "1720 / lwss c/2 = 3440
      gens to transition pixel display".

**Neighbour communication** happens through gliders crossing the tile boundary
on 8 channels (NW, N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W). That is Brice's header ("eight
glider output channels") and slide 21. Inside the tile, the signals are LWSS,
MWSS and HWSS tracks. The macro update is **synchronous**: every validation
matches the synchronous Life-like update of the model grid.

### 3.1 ON vs OFF tile (exact)

- **ON − OFF: 42 422 cells.** All of them are in the display area, bounding box
  (166..1946, 101..1881): the LWSS lanes.
- **OFF − ON: 139 cells**:
  - the 5-cell **state boat** at (569..571, 1970..1972);
  - 134 cells of the 43+43 emitter oscillators and the HWSS guns at y ≈ 102..110
    and x ≈ 1937..1945, which are in their "idle" configuration.
- **Everything else is identical**, including all rule-table and fuse objects.

## 4. Programming spec (exact; all items verified)

### 4.1 Rule bits (B0..B8, S0..S8)

To put digit *d* in the rule, **add one live cell** at the listed tile-nominal
coordinate in **every** tile, ON and OFF alike. To leave it out, add nothing.

- **What the cell does.** It completes the broken eater `o$3o$3bo$2bo!` into a
  fish-hook eater 1. The header says: *"The presence of an eater denotes
  membership of {B,S}n within the active rule set ... defective eaters burn
  cleanly."*
- **File coordinates** are these values + 5.

| d | B*d* cell | broken B eater origin | S*d* cell | broken S eater (180° rotated) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | (148, 1404) | (145, 1401) | (162, 1406) | origin (165, 1521), offset 112 |
| 7 | (148, 1414) | (145, 1411) | (162, 1416) | … offset 102 |
| 6 | (148, 1424) | (145, 1421) | (162, 1426) | … offset 92 |
| 5 | (148, 1450) | (145, 1447) | (162, 1452) | … offset 66 |
| 4 | (148, 1460) | (145, 1457) | (162, 1462) | … offset 56 |
| 3 | (148, 1470) | (145, 1467) | (162, 1472) | … offset 46 |
| 2 | (148, 1496) | (145, 1493) | (162, 1498) | … offset 20 |
| 1 | (148, 1506) | (145, 1503) | (162, 1508) | … offset 10 |
| 0 | (148, 1516) | (145, 1513) | (162, 1518) | … offset 0 |

- **Formula.** `o = [0,10,20,46,56,66,92,102,112][8−d]`, `B_d = (148, 1404+o)`,
  `S_d = (162, 1406+o)`.
  - In `metafier.py` this is `ind = 32 − 4·d` into the template
    `"b10$b10$b26$b10$b10$b26$b10$b10$b!"`. That template is placed at
    (148,1404) for B and (162,1406) for S.
  - In the Lua version it is `ind = 225 − 4·byte(d)`, 1-based.
- **Conway Life (B3/S23)** adds 3 cells: (148,1470), (162,1472), (162,1498).
  The shipped `OTCAmetapixel-{ON,OFF}-B3S23.rle` are exactly that.
- **Verification:**
  1. The Golly demos contain exactly these cells for B3/S23 (galaxy), B35/S236
     (p216) and B135/S135 (parity64). Checked cell for cell.
  2. All 18 bits were tested individually (36 bgolly runs). In a 3×3 grid, the
     centre tile, OFF for B*d* or ON for S*d*, with exactly *d* ON neighbours,
     is ON at metagen 1 when the rule contains only digit *d*. It is OFF when
     the rule contains every other digit but not *d*. 36/36 pass.
- **B0 rules work** (verified for the centre tile). Brice suggests `B0/S`, which
  makes a single isolated tile blink. Tiles outside the grid do not exist, so
  they are never born.

### 4.2 Neighbourhood programming (fuses)

- **The default is Moore**: all 8 fuse objects present. To drop a direction from
  every macro-cell's neighbourhood, **remove** the listed cells from **every**
  tile.
- **This was verified with a 3×3 grid and rule B1/S.** The centre tile is born
  iff it "hears" the single ON tile. Each removal was tested against all 8
  directions, 64 runs in total.

| Remove this object | Cells (tile-nominal) | Each tile becomes **deaf to** its … neighbour |
|---|---|---|
| NW corner LWSS | (9..12,0) (8,1) (12,1) (12,2) (8,3) (11,3) | **SE** |
| NE corner LWSS | (2047,9..12) (2046,8) (2046,12) (2045,12) (2044,8) (2044,11) | **SW** |
| SE corner LWSS | (2035,2045..2047) (2036,2044) (2036,2047) (2037,2047) (2038,2047) (2039,2044) (2039,2046) | **NW** |
| SW corner LWSS | (0,2035..2038) (1,2035) (1,2039) (2,2035) (3,2036) (3,2039) | **NE** |
| N edge block | (1001..1002, 0..1) | **N** |
| E edge block | (2046..2047, 1001..1002) | **E** |
| S edge block | (1045..1046, 2046..2047) | **S** |
| W edge block | (0..1, 1045..1046) | **W** |

- **A corner LWSS gates the tile's own output** toward that corner, as Brice
  says.
- **An edge block opens the neighbour's output toward this tile.** So it acts as
  this tile's input from that edge. Leave the eater next to the block alone.
  Brice: *"Do not remove the eater near the block."*
- **Brice's script uses a different mask convention.** Its mask is
  `N(NW,N,NE,E,SE,S,SW,W)` and it swaps nodes `F<dir>0/1`. That mask is
  therefore "output" for corners and "input" for edges. This only matters for
  neighbourhoods that are not 180°-symmetric.
- **von Neumann** = remove the 4 corner LWSSs. The `metapixel-parity64` demo
  does exactly this: 36 LWSS cells plus the 4 corner cells are missing.

## 5. Timing and macro-state readout

### 5.1 Measured behaviour

Measured on a 3×3 B3/S23 blinker grid, every generation over t = 1…70 656, and
confirmed on the Golly demos.

- **Period.** P = 35 328. Metagen *n* → *n*+1 takes exactly P generations.
- **The first cycle is "abnormal".** The fuses burn and the first neighbour
  signals are exchanged. *No* display changes during t ∈ [0, P). This matches
  the `metafier.lua` comment: *"the first cycle is abnormal, since it does not
  advance the metapattern ... at the end of 35328 ticks, the pattern is ready to
  start its first normal metageneration"*.
- **Transitions inside the readout core `[250,1750]²`**, with t mod P measured in
  cycles k ≥ 1:
  - ON→OFF: the core leaves its steady pattern at **4320** and is empty from
    **7362**. A single dying tile gives the same numbers.
  - OFF→ON: the first core cell appears at **4366**. The core equals the
    steady-ON pattern from **7408** onward.
- **The display is stable** for t mod P ∈ [7408, P) ∪ [0, 4320). That is 32 240 of 35 328
  generations, ≈91 %, for the core. Compare Brice's quoted 90 % duty cycle.
- **Displayed metagen:** `m(t) = max(0, ⌊(t − 7408)/P⌋)` for t outside
  [kP+4320, kP+7407], k ≥ 1.
- **The full 1720×1720 display** takes ≈3440 generations to switch (Brice).
  Its outer parts start changing before 4320. We did not isolate exactly when,
  because the rectangle `[166,1946]²` also contains rule-table circuitry whose
  population changes every cycle.

### 5.2 Readout values

| Tile state | Core `[250,1750]²` population |
|---|---|
| OFF (any generation, measured over 70 656 consecutive generations) | **0** |
| Steady ON (any phase) | 21 677 … 29 795. The sequence is periodic with **period 46** |
| Steady ON at t ≡ 0 (mod 46), e.g. t = k·P | **29 795** |

At **t = k·P (k ≥ 1)**, every tile's core is either empty or **bit-identical**
to the core of `OTCAmetapixel-ON.rle` at generation 0. This was verified on the
blinker grid at P, 2P, 3P and 4P.

### 5.3 Recommended readout API

1. Run to `t = (n+1)·P`.
2. For each tile, count live cells in the nominal rectangle `[250,1750]²`:
   - `0` → OFF;
   - `29 795` → ON;
   - anything else → error. Either the timing is wrong or the construction is
     broken.

   A cheaper variant probes a handful of cells known to be live in the ON
   template core at t ≡ 0 (mod 46). A stricter variant compares against the
   template bit for bit.
3. For live visualisation at arbitrary t, use thresholds: `count == 0` → OFF,
   `count ≥ 21 677` → ON, otherwise "transitioning". Report `m(t)` from the
   formula above.

## 6. Finite grids and boundaries (verified)

- **Outside the grid acts as permanently OFF.** It never sends signals and can
  never be born. The fuses at the outer border never burn, so the channels stay
  closed. Test results:
  - 1×3 `OOO` gives `.O.` then `...` (the middle tile survives on 2 neighbours;
    tiles beyond the edge are not born).
  - 3×1 behaves the same.
  - 2×2 `OO/OO` is stable forever. Its full cell state is exactly periodic
    with period P from t = P.
  - A single ON tile dies at metagen 1.
  - All-OFF stays OFF.
- **No debris escapes.** In every test and every demo (up to 9 metagens), the
  Golly bounding box stayed equal to the initial one, e.g. `-5 -5 .. 6148 6148`
  for 3×3.
- **No padding ring of OFF tiles is needed** to keep the construction clean.
  - Add a ring of OFF tiles only if you want the *model* to evolve as on an
    infinite plane. Otherwise a pattern touching the edge loses births outside
    it.
  - Example: a blinker in a 1×3 grid dies instead of oscillating.
  - Brice's tilings are full rectangles of tiles (OFF where dead). His MCL
    import also supports "null" cells with no tile (`Z`); the p216 demo is
    sparse, 4 512 tiles in a 142×119 box, and those holes behave as OFF.
- **Full-state periodicity** (SHA-256 of all cells at t = kP, k = 1…8):
  - The blinker grid is exactly periodic with period **4P** = 141 312 from
    t = P. The macro period is 2 metagens, but some internal state has period 4.
  - The t = 0 state never recurs, because of the first-cycle fuse burning.
  - An isolated OFF tile settles to 22 815 cells, periodic with period P from
    t = 2P.

## 9. Implementation spec for our Rust crates (pure Rust, no Bevy)

1. **Assets.**
   - Load `OTCAmetapixel-OFF.rle` and `-ON.rle` (unprogrammed) once and shift
     by (−5, −5).
   - Validate the populations (23 920 and 66 203) and the 2058×2058 bounding
     box.
2. **Program.** `program(tile, rule: LifeLikeRule, nbhd: MooreMask)`:
   - add the B/S cells from §4.1;
   - remove the fuse objects for excluded directions from §4.2, mapping each
     corner to its *opposite* LWSS;
   - apply the same programming to ON and OFF.
3. **Build.**
   - For an N×M boolean model, OR the programmed ON or OFF tile at
     (2048·i, 2048·j). Optionally pad with a ring of OFF tiles.
   - Prefer grid origins on multiples of 2048, aligned to quadtree level 11.
   - Optionally implement Method B (trimmed + tubs) for direct macrocell
     building. Its tests must hash identically after adding the 4 corner blocks
     at every tile corner. Note that with Method A, the outer-boundary blocks
     are present as full 2×2 blocks.
4. **Run.** Use HashLife steps of P (or powers of two summing to P). It is fully
   deterministic.
5. **Read.** Use §5.3: `macro_state(n)` reads at t = (n+1)·P. During animation
   use the `m(t)` formula and thresholds.
6. **Limits.**
   - Only outer-totalistic 2-state Moore subsets are supported. Generations,
     multistate, isotropic non-totalistic and Hex rules are not.
   - B0 works inside the grid only; outside stays dead.

**Runtime expectations.**

  1 metagen is 35 328 generations, about 1.3·10¹² cell updates.

    0.13 s for 8.

- **Our target:** within 2–5× of Golly. Give the HashLife cache ≥ 1 GB for grids
  above ~10 000 tiles. That figure is an estimate, **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Rendering.** Ask the engine for per-tile core counts. Aggregate quadtree
  populations over the `[250,1750]²` sub-square of each level-11 node, which is
  cheap with memoised node populations. Do not render cell by cell.
